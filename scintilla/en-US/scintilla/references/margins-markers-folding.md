# Margins, markers, and folding

Margins can contain line numbers, folding symbols, and debugger markers. Define each margin's type, width, mask, mouse sensitivity, and blank-area behavior; recalculate character-based widths after zoom or font changes.

Markers are line-level bit masks configured with `SCI_MARKERDEFINE`, colors, and alpha. On margin clicks, combine `SCN_MARGINCLICK` modifiers with the intended folding or debugger operation.

Folding relies on fold levels generated by the lexer. When configuring `SCI_SETPROPERTY`, fold flags, automatic folding, and persisted fold state, distinguish lexer-generated levels from user expansion state. Recalculate after reload or lexer changes so cached levels cannot become stale.
